MADNESSSIDE A · 33⅓ RPM

Heavy rock with a taste for the strange.

Probable Death or Madness is a new project by Johnny E, a singer, guitarist and songwriter based in Järvenpää, Finland.

The music is landing somewhere between Sabbath’s weight, Dio’s melody, Monster Magnet’s swagger and the darker side of Ghost and Type O Negative. There’s some Ramones directness and Clutch groove in there too.

I’ve played in bands, written songs and spent plenty of time recording and producing. Now I’m putting together three demos and looking for people who want to turn these ideas into a band.

Johnny EVocals / guitar / songwriting
